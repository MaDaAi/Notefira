Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

In short: Notefira is a free note-taking app that keeps your notebooks on your own device. The Notefira project does not run a server, does not have user accounts, and does not receive, sell or share your notebooks. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking. A few optional features contact other companies only when you choose to use them; they are described below.

Who we are

Notefira is a free, non-commercial project (“the Notefira project”, “we”) based in Ontario, Canada. For help with Notefira, please use GitHub. For privacy or legal questions, email notefirasupport@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Everything you create in Notefira is stored locally, in your browser's storage (for the web app) or in the app's data on your computer (for the desktop app). This includes:

  • your notebooks, pages, handwriting, text, images, imported PDFs, covers and templates;
  • audio recordings and any transcripts you choose to create;
  • study sets and flash cards;
  • your settings (for example theme, pens and colours);
  • if you connect Google Drive: your Google account name, email address and profile picture (used only to show which account is connected and, on this device, to remember which notebooks belong to which account so they are never mixed up), and sign-in details needed to keep the connection working.

We cannot see or access this information. It is not encrypted by Notefira, so anyone who can use your device account or browser profile may be able to read it. Your browser or operating system may delete it (for example if you clear site data), so please keep your own backups using Settings → Backup & storage.

Information we collect

The Notefira project does not collect personal information. We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, fingerprinting or tracking technologies, and Notefira does not set cookies (see the Cookie Policy).

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub, Inc. receives standard technical information such as your IP address and browser details to deliver the site and keep it secure. This is handled by GitHub under the GitHub General Privacy Statement; we do not receive visitor logs.

If you email us, we will receive your email address and whatever you choose to send, and use it only to reply.

Support on GitHub

Notefira's support, bug reports and discussions take place on GitHub (github.com/MaDaAi/Notefira). If you post an issue, discussion or comment there, it is public and is handled by GitHub under its own terms and privacy statement, using your GitHub account. Please don't post personal information or private notebook content. Security reports sent through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting are visible only to the maintainers.

Optional features that use other services

These features are off until you use them. When you do, your device communicates directly with the company named; we are not in the middle and do not receive the data.

Google Drive sync

If you choose Sign in with Google, Google's sign-in service is loaded from accounts.google.com and you are asked to grant Notefira these permissions:

  • drive.file — access only to files Notefira creates in your Google Drive (a “Notefira Sync” folder). Notefira cannot see your other Drive files.
  • openid, email, profile — your name, email address and profile picture, used only to show which account is connected.

Your notebooks are then copied between your device and your own Google Drive over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. On the web, the access token is kept only in memory and in the current browser tab's session storage. In the desktop app, the long-lived sign-in token is stored only in your operating system's secure keychain; if no secure keychain is available, it is not stored at all and you sign in again each time you open Notefira. Notefira never writes Google sign-in tokens to its local database. Signing out in Notefira revokes the token; you can also remove Notefira's access at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps & services. Information in your Drive is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Notefira's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. That information is used only to sync your notebooks with your own Drive and to show which account is connected; it is not used for advertising, not sold, and not transferred to anyone else.

Handwriting and text recognition (search in images and handwriting)

The first time you use text recognition, Notefira downloads the open-source Tesseract recognition engine and English language data (about 15 MB) from the public jsDelivr content network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). jsDelivr receives a normal download request, including your IP address. Recognition itself runs on your device; your pages and images are not uploaded.

Live transcription of audio recordings

Audio recordings are stored only on your device. Live transcription is off by default. If you turn it on, Notefira uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Some browsers send the audio to their provider to convert it to text — for example Google in Chrome, Microsoft in Edge, or Apple in Safari — under that provider's privacy policy. If you do not want that, leave live transcription off.

Links you open

If you open a web link from a notebook, Notefira asks first and then opens it in your browser. The website you visit is responsible for its own privacy practices.

Children

Notefira is not directed to children under 13 and the Notefira project does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Using Google Drive sync requires a Google account, which is subject to Google's own age rules.

Your choices and rights

Because your notebooks stay on your device, you are in control: you can export, back up or delete them at any time inside the app, or delete all Notefira data by clearing this site's data in your browser (or uninstalling the desktop app). Data synced to Google Drive can be managed or deleted in your Drive.

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation or US state privacy laws may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information about you. Since the Notefira project does not hold personal information about Notefira users, we will usually have nothing to provide, but you are welcome to contact us with any question or request.

Security

Notefira's code is open for review. Connections to Google use HTTPS, and Notefira only opens web and email links after asking you. No method of storing data is perfectly secure, so please protect your device with a password and keep backups.

Changes to this policy

If Notefira's handling of information changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Previous versions are available in the project's public source history.

Contact

Privacy questions, requests or concerns: notefirasupport@gmail.com. For general help, use GitHub Discussions. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local data protection authority.
