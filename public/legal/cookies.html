Cookie Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

In short: Notefira does not set cookies and does not track you. It uses your browser's storage only to save your notebooks and settings on your device, which is necessary for the app to work. That's why there is no cookie banner.

Cookies

Notefira itself sets no cookies — no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no cookies to identify you.

Storage that Notefira needs to work

Like any app that works offline, Notefira saves information in your browser (or, for the desktop app, on your computer). This information stays on your device and is never sent to the Notefira project.

NameTypePurposeKept until
notefiraIndexedDB databaseYour notebooks, pages, files, recordings, study sets and settingsYou delete them or clear site data
nf-onboardedLocal storageRemembers that you've passed the welcome screenYou clear site data
nf-themeLocal storageApplies your light/dark theme before the app finishes loadingYou clear site data
notefira-journalLocal storageShort-term crash recovery for unsaved changesCleared after each successful save
notefira-gtokenSession storageTemporary Google Drive access while you are signed in (only if you use Drive sync)The browser tab is closed, or you sign out
notefira-app-…, notefira-runtime-v1Cache storage (service worker)Lets Notefira open offline and keeps the text-recognition language data after its first downloadA newer version replaces it, or you clear site data

Because this storage is strictly necessary to provide the features you use and is not used for tracking, consent banners are not required for it under the privacy rules we are aware of. If Notefira ever adds anything that is not strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

Other companies

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google's sign-in window runs on Google's own website and may use Google's cookies under the Google cookie policy. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes technical data as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

How to remove stored data

Back up anything you want to keep first (Settings → Backup & storage). Then clear the site data for this website in your browser's settings (usually under Privacy → Cookies and site data), or uninstall the desktop app.
